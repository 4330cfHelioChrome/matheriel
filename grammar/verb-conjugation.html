Verb Conjugation

p

h2

The base form of all verbs and adjectives is ÷ after the stem.
Example: π!÷ (stem + -다) = eat

Pre-final endings indicating tense come before the stem.
Example: <π!÷ (past tense pre-final ending + stem + -다) = ate
